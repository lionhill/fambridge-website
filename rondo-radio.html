A Fambridge digital experience

Sometimes a song is another way of being close.

Rondo Radio grew from our own love of music—and from the simple idea that familiar sounds can connect people across distance.

The story

A familiar song can carry more than music.

It can bring back a place, a memory, a person or a moment that seemed far away a few seconds earlier.

Rondo Radio was created as a simple way to discover and enjoy thoughtfully selected radio stations from around the world. In its own quiet way, it reflects the same idea behind Fambridge: shared experiences can make distance feel smaller.

“Whether the people we care about are in the same room or thousands of kilometres away, music gives us something we can still share.”

Rondo Radio is developed by Fambridge Trading Inc. as a digital product alongside our communications and connected-device business.
